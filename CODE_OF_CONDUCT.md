# Community Code of Conduct

This project welcomes thoughtful technical discussion focused on safety, learning, and reproducible engineering.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- critique code and evidence rather than people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or unwanted sexual attention;
- protect private account, credential, and vulnerability information;
- disclose conflicts of interest and avoid deceptive profit claims;
- respect platform rules, intellectual property, and the project's licensing status.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when behavior threatens people, security, privacy, legal compliance, or constructive collaboration.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's established contact channel. Report vulnerabilities through the private process in `SECURITY.md`.

This policy does not create an obligation to accept contributions, provide trading support, or publish private security details.
